What actually makes this different

Most counseling credentials are written purely for theory. Ours are built by practicing psychiatrists, psychologists, and educators for real-world impact.

Faculty

Faculty who still practice

Every core instructor supervises real clinical and educational casework alongside teaching — the curriculum gets updated by what's actually showing up in schools and clinics, not just literature.

Schedule

Built around a working schedule

Live sessions and self-paced modules are scheduled around the professional calendar educators and clinicians already work within.

Recognition

HEC-approved, not HEC-adjacent

Presented jointly by Health Services Academy (HSA) and PIMH, with 18 + 2 credit hours mapped directly to Higher Education Commission requirements.

Fieldwork

Practice, not just theory

Supervised casework and real case studies run through the program — by the final module, you're consulting on live situations, not hypotheticals.

How the diploma actually unfolds

Four stages, from registration to certification — each one online, each one tracked in your student dashboard.

Register & verify

Create your student account, upload your fee receipt, and unlock your LMS portal.

Core modules

Foundational psychology and counseling modules, delivered live and on-demand.

Assessments & practicum

Applied casework, timed module quizzes, and faculty-graded assignments.

Diploma awarded

A verifiable, HEC-approved diploma — plus a certificate you can download directly from your dashboard.

Every session lives in one place

Recorded lectures, live class links, assignments, and your grade history — all inside the same dashboard you'll log into on day one, not scattered across email threads and shared drives.
