What actually makes this different

Most counseling credentials are written for clinicians. This one is written for people standing in front of a classroom.

Faculty

Faculty who still practice

Every core instructor supervises real casework alongside teaching — the curriculum gets updated by what's actually showing up in schools, not just literature.

Schedule

Built around a teaching calendar

Live sessions are scheduled around the academic year educators already work within, not a generic term calendar borrowed from a psychology department.

Recognition

HEC-aligned, not HEC-adjacent

Credit hours and program structure are mapped directly to Higher Education Commission requirements, so the diploma counts where you need it to.

Fieldwork

Practice, not just theory

Supervised fieldwork and real case studies run through both semesters — by the final module, you're consulting on live situations, not hypotheticals.

How the diploma actually unfolds

Four stages, from application to certification — each one online, each one tracked in your student dashboard.

Apply & enroll

Submit your application, confirm your seat, and get dashboard access within days.

Semester one

Foundational counseling theory and educational psychology, delivered live and on-demand.

Semester two & practicum

Applied casework, supervised practice, and your final assessed project.

Diploma awarded

A verifiable, HEC-aligned diploma — plus a certificate you can share directly from your dashboard.

Every session lives in one place

Recorded lectures, live class links, assignments, and your grade history — all inside the same dashboard you'll log into on day one, not scattered across email threads and shared drives.
